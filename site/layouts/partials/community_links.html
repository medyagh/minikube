Meetings

We hold regular community meetings where you can connect with the minikube team. To get calendar invites for our meetings, please subscribe to the minikube-dev mailing list.

  • Office Hours: Bi-weekly office hours, Mondays @ 11am PST.
  • Triage Party: All community members are welcome and encouraged to join and help us triage minikube!

Community

Join the conversation and ask questions in our community channels.

  • Slack: Chat with other minikube users and developers in the #minikube channel.
  • Slack Invitation: Use this invitation link to join the Kubernetes Slack workspace.

Contributing

minikube is a Kubernetes #sig-cluster-lifecycle project. Development takes place on GitHub. Check out our Contributing Guide to get started.
